1. Who We Are

Emili is a video generation and social media publishing service for animal shelters and rescue organizations. We are not affiliated with TikTok, Meta, Google, or any other social media platform. Our contact email is hind@emili.net.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate and publish adoption videos to your connected accounts, only when you explicitly request it.
  • To maintain an active OAuth session so you do not need to re-authorize on every use.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or analytics.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party.

4. TikTok Data

When you connect your TikTok account through the App, we receive an access token and a refresh token via TikTok's official OAuth 2.0 flow. These tokens are:

  • Stored locally on the server running the App (in a tokens.json file or environment variables).
  • Used exclusively to call TikTok's Content Posting API on your behalf.
  • Never shared with or sold to any third party.
  • Revocable at any time from your TikTok Account → Security Settings → Connected Apps.

5. Data Retention

OAuth tokens are retained only as long as necessary to provide the service. You may request deletion of all stored data at any time by contacting us at hind@emili.net or visiting our Data Deletion page.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect stored tokens and credentials. All communications between the App and social platform APIs are encrypted via HTTPS. However, no system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
